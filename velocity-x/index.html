About Velocity X

BUILT FOR THOSE
WHO ARRIVE FIRST.

Velocity X began in 2012 with a single bay in Vijayawada and a stubborn belief: that buying an extraordinary car should feel as extraordinary as driving one. Today our four flagship showrooms house some of the rarest machines in the country.

Every car is sourced, inspected and re-commissioned in-house. Every client gets a specialist, a detailer and a direct line to the person who sold them the car — for as long as they own it.
